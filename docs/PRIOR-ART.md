# Prior art and claim boundary

Commodity or established categories include Prometheus/Grafana pipeline monitoring, Telestream Vidchecker, Interra BATON, Shade, EditShare, logistics pre-miss alerts, and software-delivery error-budget writing. Media supply-chain orchestrators include SDVI Rally, Dalet Flex, Vidispine, and Ateme.

Required project wording:

> Our documented search found standard pipeline monitoring stacks, automated QC and deliverables products, predictive pre-miss alerting in logistics, and existing writing on applying error budgets to delivery. We did not confirm whether media supply chain orchestrators predict deadline risk, and we do not claim they cannot.

SLATE competes on a real instrumented media pipeline, partner depth, and execution—not a claim that schedule forecasting or observability is new.
