# Asset and data rights

SLATE contains no third-party film clips, music, logos, fonts, or training data.
FFmpeg generates the three-second color test source and sine tone at runtime.
All benchmark cases, delivery names, failures, screenshots, and telemetry used in
the judging build are synthetic and authored for this project.

Product and technology names appear only as factual attribution. Their software
is used under the licenses shipped by the respective projects and distributions;
SLATE itself is Apache-2.0.
